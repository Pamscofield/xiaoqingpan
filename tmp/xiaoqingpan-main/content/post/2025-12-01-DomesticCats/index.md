Oh my god this is a science cover paper! Finally, people have investigated what I am interested. 

Sequencing mtDNA, Martino *et al* have found that domestic cats (*Felis catus*) are most genetically similar to African wildcats (*F. lybica lybica*) ~2000 years ago, which is presently distributed across North Africa and the Near East.
